Science • 3D Digestion Journey

Scroll to move food from mouth to elimination while the animation evolves in real-time.

Progress: 0%

1) Mouth

Chewing and saliva break food into smaller pieces, beginning digestion.

2) Esophagus

Wave-like contractions (peristalsis) push food down to the stomach.

3) Stomach

Acid and enzymes churn food into a semi-liquid mixture called chyme.

4) Small Intestine

Nutrients are absorbed into the bloodstream through millions of villi.

5) Large Intestine

Water and minerals are absorbed while the remaining material compacts.

6) End of Journey

Waste exits the body. You have completed the digestive system pathway.
